GLITCH CLICK BEATS - Max for Live audio effect (unfrozen)
1. Put GlitchClickBeats.amxd AND gcb_orbit.js, gcb_grid.js, gcb_adsr.js in ONE folder
   inside your Live User Library (e.g. Presets/Audio Effects/Max Audio Effect/GlitchClickBeats/).
2. Drag the .amxd onto an audio track and press play in Live (it follows the transport).
If a visual shows a red "js error: ..." line, that text is the cause. Max console messages beginning "jsui:" also help.
gen_engine.genexpr = full gen~ source (reference).
